C · Point 6, part 1

Progress as value moments, not setup steps

No 0 % meter and no checklist of settings. The path counts things Coanda did for the person. Each milestone gets one short success line when it first happens, then the path goes away for good after the third.

Goals savedSetup, day 1
First choice in one clickA question answered with a button

After the path: from day 2, one quiet "proof line" at rest, at most once a day, never while Claude works. You can see it in step 6 of part A.

D · Point 6, part 2

With Coanda vs without: what to measure

Coanda claims two things: it catches wrong turns, and it protects the goal you chose. Each KPI below can be counted on your own machine from session transcripts and the band's hooks, with nothing sent outside.

KPIWhat it showsHow to count itBetter is
Corrections per session · north starWrong turns that got through: you had to redirect ClaudeYour messages that undo or redirect ("no", "stop", "not that", "revert"), plus turns you interrupted↓ lower
ReworkThe cost of a wrong turnEdits reverted in the same session, and git revert / git checkout -- calls↓ lower
Time to decideHow fast you answer Claude's questionsSeconds from Claude's question to your answer, by button or typed↓ lower
Turns on the PrimaryProtecting the goalShare of turns whose work matches today's Primary (Claude rates each turn against goals.md, so this is inferred)↑ higher
Primary done rateWhether the day landsDays the Primary is marked done ÷ days one was set↑ higher
Topic switches per dayDriftNew sessions or prompts on a topic outside the Goal and Lanes↓ lower
Still on at day 7Whether people keep it (for other users)The plugin is still on, and at least one question was answered by button in week 1↑ higher

The test: two weeks, same person

  1. Week 1, Coanda off but logging. The hooks count everything above. The band shows nothing. This is the baseline.
  2. Week 2, Coanda on. Same counts.
  3. Compare per session, not per week, since the amount of work changes. Keep notes on unusual days (a deadline, a new project).
  4. For other users later: the same pair of weeks, or half the questions shown in the band and half left in the chat.
